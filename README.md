## Project Overview: Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
This repository contains a data analysis project focused on driving revenue growth and improving product performance for Bright Coffee Shop.

Using a comprehensive historical dataset of daily transactional sales, this project aims to extract data-driven, actionable insights to support the company's newly appointed CEO in strategic decision-making.

## Key Objectives
• Analyze historical sales data to identify top-performing products and revenue trends.
• Uncover actionable insights to optimize the product mix and boost daily profitability.
• Provide strategic recommendations to assist executive leadership in business growth.

## Problem Statement
Bright Coffee Shop is facing stagnation in daily revenue and uneven product performance across its categories. Despite capturing a wealth of transactional details, the business has historically operated without data-driven strategies.
The newly appointed CEO has set a critical mission to accelerate company growth, the executive team currently lacks visibility into the specific drivers behind customer behavior, peak sales periods, and underperforming menu items.

Without deep analytics, the business risks:
• Inefficient Inventory Management: Overstocking slow-moving ingredients while facing stockouts on high-demand items.
• Missed Revenue Opportunities: Failing to leverage optimal pricing models and promotional timing during peak customer traffic hours.
• Stagnant Profit Margins: Investing marketing effort and shelf space into low-margin products instead of high-value revenue drivers.

This project solves this business problem by transforming raw historical transaction records into a diagnostic analytics framework, identifying exactly when, what, and how customers buy to maximize daily profitability.

## Business Questions
To solve the problem statement, this analysis focuses on answering the following critical business questions for the CEO:
• Sales Trends: What are the daily, weekly, and monthly revenue trends? Are there specific days or months driving the highest sales?
• Peak Performance: What are the peak operating hours during the day, and how should staffing or inventory adapt to these timeframes?
• Product Insights: Which menu items are the top revenue drivers, and which items are underperforming and require optimization or removal?
• Customer Behavior: What is the average transaction value, and are there visible purchasing patterns or product pairings that can be leveraged for upselling?

## Tools Used
The project utilizes a modern data stack to process raw records, build models, and present executive insights:
• Databricks: Used for scalable data processing, data cleaning, and exploratory data analysis (EDA) and dashboard creation using SQL.
• Microsoft Excel: Used for Pivot tables and Dashboard creation.
• Microsoft PowerPoint: Used to build the final executive presentation and communicate actionable insights to the CEO.
